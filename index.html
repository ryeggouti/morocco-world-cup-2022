Morocco Qatar 2022 Semi-final In numbers

How did Morocco become the first African team to reach a World Cup semi-final? Fewer shots, better chances, and a defence that got stronger as its opponents did. I analysed free match data from all 64 games to find out.

    Finding 01

    Fewer shots, better chances.

    Spain and Portugal out-shot Morocco. Switch to xG, which measures how likely each shot was to score, and the picture flips.

    Finding 02

    Defending the box.

    Every shot Morocco's opponents took. In the knockouts they often reached the box, but mostly for low-quality chances. Spain and Portugal took 24 shots combined and scored none.

      Finding 03

      Beating the odds.

      En-Nesyri scored 2 goals from 1.05 xG. Ziyech scored from 10 shots worth only 0.31 xG. As a team, Morocco scored – goals from – xG.

      Finding 04

      Stronger when it got harder.

      xG per shot conceded measures how dangerous the average shot against Morocco was. In the group stage, Morocco ranked only 28th of 32 teams. Against Spain, Portugal, France and Croatia, it dropped sharply.

      How I built it

      Free data, Python, and a lot of checking.

      Event data for every 2022 World Cup match from StatsBomb Open Data, analysed in Python (pandas, matplotlib, mplsoccer) in Google Colab.

      The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Chart.js, hosted on GitHub Pages. I used AI tools to help write some of the code; I chose the questions, checked the results and wrote the findings.
